About Me

Hi, I'm Momina. I'm a computer science student currently exploring networking and IT infrastructure. I'm especially interested in understanding how systems communicate across networks and how the internet works behind the scenes.

I originally became interested in technology through web design and front-end development. Building websites helped me understand how things work on the surface of the internet, but over time that curiosity led me to explore what happens behind the scenes. Things like networks, servers, and how systems communicate with each other.

Right now I'm focused on learning networking fundamentals while preparing for the CompTIA Network+ certification. I'm also curious about cybersecurity and how network systems can be secured and protected as I continue exploring different areas of IT.

This website is where I document what I'm learning and share notes as I study different networking concepts. While my main focus right now is networking, I still enjoy creative projects and web design, which is why I like building and experimenting with my own site.

If you're interested in the topics I'm learning, feel free to explore the notes section. If you'd like to see more about my background and experience, you can also check out my resume.
